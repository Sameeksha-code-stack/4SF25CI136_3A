Java is a high-level, interpreted programming language known for its simple, readable syntax and versatility. It supports multiple programming paradigms, including procedural, object-oriented, and functional programming. Java’s extensive standard library and vast ecosystem of third-party packages make it suitable for a wide range of applications, from web development and data analysis to artificial intelligence and automation. Its beginner-friendly nature, combined with strong community support, has made Java one of the most popular and widely used languages in the world today.
